Setup - Search Themes

Latest News only

Latest News Search Themes

Use this page to decide what KestrelIQ should look for when it searches news for account names. These themes are added to each company query so the feed focuses on business signals instead of unrelated general mentions.

WHAT THIS CONTROLSLatest News searches for account names plus these themes, such as CEO, earnings, launch, partnership, AI, customer, regulatory, outage, and layoffs.
WHEN TO CUSTOMISEAdd words that matter to your leadership rhythm, such as automation, GenAI, contact center, CX, outsourcing, self-service, migration, or transformation.
HOW TO USE RESULTSAfter saving, refresh feeds. Theme Counts will show how many articles each account produced for every theme, and you can click a count to read matching articles.

Interests use a broader baseline search so topics like Claude, ChatGPT, agentic AI, or CX trends do not become overly company-finance focused. This setup applies only to Latest News account searches.
